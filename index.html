FAQ

Common questions.

Quick answers about our services and how to log a support request.

What kind of IT help do you offer?

We offer IT support, network solutions, computer services, software support, system maintenance and business IT solutions.

Who do you support?

Individuals and home offices, small and growing businesses, and teams that need everyday IT support.

How do I log a support request?

Use the Get Support button to open the ticket page. Fill in the ticket form, or download the NVT Tracker Excel workbook if you are on a desktop computer.

What should I do if the problem is critical?

Call the NovaTech Service Desk on +27 (0)11 555 0142 straight away, then log the ticket. The service desk is available Mon–Fri, 07:00–19:00.

What should I include in my ticket?

Your name, your department, the request category and a short description of the issue. Clear detail helps us pick the right priority and technician.
